# Attribution errors in LLM-generated Quranic and Hadith citations

Generated by `run_analysis.py`. No source data was modified.

**Scope.** No train/dev/test restriction: the implicit condition was never released and has no split, so the explicit side is taken in full too and the two conditions are counted identically.

**Misattributed** = text label correct or corrupted AND at least one supplied attribution component (claimed source or isnad) labelled incorrect.

**Validation gate.** Implicit, share of the model's citations: Fanar-1-9B 14.9% correct-text-misattributed / 6.0% corrupted-misattributed; ALLaM-7B 9.2% / 3.2%. Both reproduce exactly.
